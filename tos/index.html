02

Rescales, Variants & Revisions

  • A rescale is the conversion of an existing 16:9 thumbnail into a 1:1 icon, and nothing else. It does not work in reverse: an icon cannot be rescaled into a thumbnail. Turning an icon into a 16:9 thumbnail is a new thumbnail at full price.
  • All rescales are paid, regardless of difficulty.
  • A variant is a small change to an existing artwork (pose, character, item, color) and is charged as listed on the pricing page.
  • Variants that only change text or numbers are free.
  • Up to 2 rounds of minor adjustments are included per artwork (small position, color or effect tweaks).
  • Major revisions or concept changes after approval are charged separately.
